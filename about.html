At the heart of my approach

Fresh eyes, real headway — it is at the intersection of three circles that reinvention emerges

01
Letting possibilities emerge

The potential of the situation

Rather than imposing a plan through sheer willpower, we learn to read what is already there: the stakes, the networks, the invisible resources. We build on the favorable dynamics already at work. In uncertain terrain, exploring beats charging ahead — the point is to act more opportunely, by first spotting the natural propensity of things.

Favorite authorsFrançois Jullien, Saras Sarasvathy, Karl E. Weick, Clayton Christensen
02
Acting from what is truly yours

The uniqueness of each person

Every situation is decoded by a unique individual, and no two people see the same footholds. Our work uses your singular reading of your experience as a resource: revisiting your journey to turn trials into strengths, clarifying the values silently guiding your choices, and aligning what you think, feel, and aspire to.

Favorite authorsRichard Barrett, Carl Rogers, Michael White, Dan P. McAdams
03
Shifting the system

Relational dynamics

A decision-maker rarely acts alone: your impact depends on what flows between you and others — and causality often runs in circles. The way out of "more of the same" lies in a subtle shift in the rules of the game, and in developing the signals of trust that let others speak frankly, propose, and disagree.

Favorite authorsPaul Watzlawick, Gregory Bateson, Amy Edmondson, Nagarjuna
