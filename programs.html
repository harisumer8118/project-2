The journeys

Exploratory meeting

A first one-hour conversation to understand your situation in detail, and clarify the framework and terms of the coaching.

« What is your situation? »

« What have you already tried? What would you like to achieve? »

« What experience do you have with coaching? »

Agreement

Together we define the objectives and the success criteria. I commit to the method and the quality of the process, to reach your goals within a limited number of sessions.

« Which SMART goals should we prioritize? »

« How will we concretely see that you have reached them? »

« What do you want to walk away with in a few months? »

The journeys

Individual and group formats, tailored to the pace and stakes of your situation.

For you, one-on-one
Individual journey
  • 8 one-hour sessions
  • In person, by video — or walking!
  • Over 4 to 6 months, at the pace that suits you
For your team / organization
Group journeys
  • Custom-designed formats
  • Workshops, seminars, co-development, keynotes
  • Duration and pace defined together, based on your goals
