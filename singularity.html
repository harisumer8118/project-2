At the heart of my approach — it is at the intersection of three circles that reinvention emerges

My approach combines Coaching and Innovation by exploring their 3 shared levers — of oneself, one's role, one's organization.

1 — The potential of the situation

Letting possibilities emerge

Read what is already there — stakes, networks, invisible resources — and build on favorable dynamics. In uncertain terrain, exploring lets you act more opportunely, with less effort.

2 — The uniqueness of each person

Acting from what is truly yours

Revisit your journey to turn trials into strengths, clarify your values, align thought, feeling, and aspirations — and rebuild the narrative that lets you reinvent yourself.

Shifting the system

My favorite authors

01
Letting possibilities emerge

The potential of the situation

Rather than imposing a plan through sheer willpower, we learn to read what is already there: the stakes, the networks, the invisible resources.

We build on the favorable dynamics already at work. In uncertain terrain, exploring beats charging ahead. The point is not to act faster or harder, but more opportunely — by first spotting the natural propensity of things.

Starting from your means and treating the unexpected as a resource lets you move forward when the initial goal seems out of reach. Small steps, and noticing how signals connect, bring forth a sense of meaning that justifies and encourages action.

Favorite authorsFrançois Jullien, Saras Sarasvathy, Karl E. Weick, Clayton Christensen
02
Acting from what is truly yours

The uniqueness of each person

Every situation is decoded by a unique individual, and no two people see the same footholds.

Our work will use your singular reading of your experiences as a resource: revisiting your journey to turn trials into strengths, clarifying the values silently guiding your choices, and aligning what you think, what you feel, and what you aspire to, so your action gains impact.

Strength is not about having a single identity, but about knowing how to inhabit several — and rebuilding together the narrative that lets you reinvent yourself in full coherence.

Favorite authorsRichard Barrett, Carl Rogers, Michael White, Dan P. McAdams
03
Shifting the system

Relational dynamics

A decision-maker rarely acts alone: your impact depends on what flows between you and others — and causality often runs in circles.

What locks a system in place rarely comes from a lack of effort, but from the same attempted solution repeated over and over. The way out lies in a subtle shift in the rules of the game, to avoid "more of the same."

The lever also moves from what you decide to what you make possible — by developing the signals of trust that allow others to speak frankly, to propose, to disagree — without ever excusing you from setting a direction, and sometimes owning its discomfort.

Favorite authorsPaul Watzlawick, Gregory Bateson, Amy Edmondson, Nagarjuna
